# Pixel Office guide

- **What it is:** a living isometric pixel office. On this hosted site it runs as a simulation: staff wander, work, take breaks and change rooms by themselves. It does not show live coding sessions.
- **Rooms:** main office, corridor, meeting room, server room and cafeteria. Click a door, the floor plan or a room tab to go there; the boss follows.
- **Meeting room:** click its door and the whole squad comes. The first 8 sit; anyone left over stands along the front. The boss has the larger burgundy chair at the head of the table.
- **Server room:** click the yellow cables to re-seat them, the core switch to reboot it, or the crash cart to run diagnostics. The boss walks over and a speech bubble shows the result.
- **Time:** there is a day and a night look. There are no seasons.
- **The squad:** Principal Data Scientist (architecture gate and final sign-off), Senior Data Scientist (pod lead, methodology), Data Engineer (data, features, storage), Data Analyst (EDA, error slices), ML Engineer (models, serving, server room), QA and Model Critic (audits, blocks bad releases), Research Analyst (papers, baselines).
- **Ask AI:** answers any request; it can load playbooks (skills) for specific kinds of work.

Keep answers short. If something is not implemented, say so plainly instead of guessing.
