# Explain a concept

1. One-sentence definition in plain words.
2. A concrete analogy or small example.
3. How it works, in short numbered steps.
4. Two common mistakes or misconceptions.
5. One question the reader can use to check their understanding.

Match depth to the asker: a beginner gets everyday words, an expert gets precise terms. Offer to go deeper at the end.
